# Ghost-VPA: Scope, Boundaries and Foundational Assumptions

**Project:** Ghost-VPA — Bank-Controlled Honeypot VPAs  
**Document:** `docs/scope-and-assumptions.md`  

> **Implementation status (17 Nov 2026):** This document contains intended scope and assumptions, not a statement that those capabilities exist. The current application is simulation-only. It has no live bank/UPI/IMPS/NPCI integration, no production GL mapping, no valid comparative benchmark, and no authenticated case-action identity. Case mutations and benchmark results are disabled. RBI September 2026 KYC amendment directions are a draft proposal.

---

## 1. Project In-Scope Boundaries

1. **Simulated Payment Rails:**
   - Multi-bank UPI/IMPS simulator generating payment attempt events (`ReqValAdd` and `ReqPay` lifecycle equivalents).
   - Simulates 4 distinct synthetic commercial and co-operative banks: `HDFX`, `ICIX`, `SBINX`, `AXSX`.
   - Supports realistic event rates, transaction bursts, latency delays, and out-of-order event arrivals.

2. **Canary Honeypot Registry:**
   - Bank-designated regulatory honeypot VPAs explicitly marked as non-routable synthetic canary endpoints.
   - Bank-controlled internal GL suspense canary mapping under the Draft RBI Annex III 60-day SOP.
   - Real-time $O(1)$ canary lookup on incoming transactions.

3. **Event Ingestion & Immediate Alert Engine:**
   - Sub-15ms ingestion pipeline triggering immediate `REVIEW_REQUIRED` status on remitter accounts targeting canaries.
   - Zero funds movement logic: payments directed at canaries are intercepted at the CBS sidecar level with zero debit/credit ledger movement.

4. **Temporal Graph & Motif Analytics:**
   - Directed temporal multigraph tracking accounts, canaries, bank entities, and device tokens across a configurable 72-hour sliding window.
   - Algorithmic detection of rapid fan-in (mule aggregation), rapid fan-out (layering dispersion), high-velocity chains, and shared upstream feeder accounts.
   - Canary-Seeded Label Propagation (CS-LP) anchoring high-confidence seed labels ($Y=1.0$) and propagating risk backward along temporal inbound edges.

5. **Benchmarking & Machine Learning:**
   - 100,000-node synthetic financial transaction network.
   - Baseline: Tabular LightGBM (transaction-only features).
   - Challenger: Graph-Enhanced LightGBM with temporal motifs and topological features.
   - Exploratory: PyTorch Geometric temporal GNN evaluation.
   - Measurement of Precision, Recall, PR-AUC, False Positive Rate (FPR) reduction, and inference latency.

6. **Analyst Workbench & D3 Network Explorer:**
   - Single Page Application in React 18, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
   - Force-directed D3 graph visualization with bounded ego-network rendering (max 50-100 nodes per view).
   - Case management dashboard tracking the RBI 60-day SOP SLA countdown timer, 20-day customer notice window, and evidence dossier.
   - 3 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) tiers: Analyst, Supervisor, Read-Only Auditor.

7. **Threat Intelligence Export:**
   - Synthetic OASIS STIX 2.1 JSON bundle export format for cross-bank threat sharing.

## 2. Inviolable Scope Exclusions (Out of Scope)

To preserve regulatory compliance and operational safety, the following are strictly prohibited:
- **No Live Payment Switch Modification:** Zero changes to NPCI core UPI switch, ISO 8583 switch specs, or UPI Central Mapper.
- **No Live Production Banking Connectivity:** Zero connections to production Core Banking Systems (CBS), live PSP switches, or live payment gateways.
- **No Real Fund Transfers:** Zero live penny-drop tests (₹1 or ₹10 transfers on real UPI rails), zero suspense ledger cash diversion, zero live UPI collect requests.
- **No Synthetic Customer KYC Generation:** Strictly zero creation of fictitious Aadhaar, PAN, voter IDs, or forged passbooks (prohibited under PMLA 2002 and RBI KYC Master Directions).
- **No Illicit Intelligence Scraping:** Zero scraping of private Telegram/WhatsApp syndicate groups, dark-web credential dumps, or stolen databases.
- **No Automatic Guilt or Instant Account Freezes:** A canary interaction flags the account for human operational review (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`). Formal holds and outreach must be supervised under statutory RBI guidelines.

## 3. Foundational Assumptions

1. **Regulatory Sandbox Context:** The prototype evaluates the operational viability of canary VPAs within the framework of the RBI September 2026 Draft KYC Amendment Directions (Annex III SOP on Suspected Money Mule Accounts).
2. **Bank-Controlled Placement:** In production, honeypots are placed in public or restricted channels through authorised bank threat-intelligence procedures, or via consented surrender by customers during the 20-day explanation window. In the prototype, all interactions are deterministically generated by the simulation engine.
3. **Synthetic Identity Assumption:** All accounts, bank names, customer tokens, IP subnets, and transaction IDs are completely synthetic and pseudonymous.
