## Acceptance gate before deployment or external performance claims

- Implement trusted authentication and authorization for all case actions.
- Make ingestion, DB, graph, and publication behavior idempotent and recoverable.
- Verify graph math and event semantics on independent, time-aware datasets.
- Validate STIX and keep FIU/NCRP outputs preview-only until authorized integration exists.
- Obtain bank, legal, regulatory, security, accounting, and rail approvals for any real endpoint or GL design.
- Keep zero-real-funds invariant and synthetic identifiers in all demonstrations and tests.
- Publish no model metrics until a reproducible, graph-based, chronological experiment has passed independent review.
